Welcome to QuickKit

A free toolkit for After Effects and Premiere Pro. Three steps and you are set up.

  1. 1
    Run the installer Double-click scripts\install.bat in the QuickKit folder. No admin rights needed.
  2. 2
    Restart the app Close After Effects or Premiere Pro completely, then open it again.
  3. 3
    Open the panel Go to Window > Extensions > QuickKit.

Open this again any time with the help button.
